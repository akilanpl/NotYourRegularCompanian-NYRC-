# NYRC product direction

NYRC means Not Your Regular Companion. Useful when you need him. Alive when you don’t.

A small, intelligent desktop presence: graphite surfaces, dark glass display, ceramic shell, restrained eyes and status indicator. Original procedural SVG art replaces temporary inherited rasters.

Local commands are the primary interaction. Optional user-owned AI helps with interpretation. Sensitive actions pause for Allow once or Deny. Pocket, Calendar, durable reminders, aliases and modes share the assistant runtime. Product name is distinct from the user-chosen instance name.

Advanced holds developer diagnostics and the virtual body. The shared Body API is authoritative for semantic reactions across desktop and external devices. Physical hardware remains untested.
